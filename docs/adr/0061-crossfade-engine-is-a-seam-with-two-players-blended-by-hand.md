# 0061: Crossfade is an engine behind a seam; the blend is two ExoPlayers driven by hand

Date: 2026-11-14 · Status: proposed

**Context.** Phase 6 needs songs to overlap. Media3 has no crossfade: one ExoPlayer joins items gaplessly (ADR 0020 keeps a window of the queue on it) and cannot play two items at once. CLAUDE.md asks that a decision point sits behind an interface and that the current path stays as an alternative. The session, the notification, the queue window (0020), jumps (0032), previous (0033) and the saved queue (0036) must keep seeing one player and one position.

**Decision.** `CrossfadeEngine` (`attach`, `invalidate`, `onPlayingChanged`, `release`) is driven by `PlaybackEngine`, which is its `CrossfadeHost` (`activePlayer`, `planNext`, `fadeStarted`, `fadeFinished`). `SinglePlayerEngine` is the phase 1 to 5 path: it does nothing, Media3 joins songs. `TwoPlayerCrossfadeEngine` blends two ExoPlayers; one Hilt `@Provides` line in `PlaybackModule` picks the factory, so both can be benchmarked. Blend: the host's player plays the outgoing song; when `planNext` says fade, the next song is preloaded on the spare player (silent, paused); when the outgoing song is `fade` from its end the spare starts at volume 0, the host swaps to it (`MediaSession.setPlayer`, a new `QueueAwarePlayer`, a new `QueueWindowSync` grown around the one song by `adopt`, no restart), the outgoing player drops the items after its own, and both volumes follow `FadeCurve` of the incoming player's position (not a timer, so a pause or seek cannot desync them). The outgoing player then becomes the spare. Only the active player holds audio focus. Not ready at the start moment, or a hard cut: nothing happens, Media3's own gapless join runs. Any queue change, seek, jump or setting change calls `invalidate`, which cuts a running fade hard.

**Consequences.** The session shows the incoming song from the start of the fade with its own position and length. A second ExoPlayer is built the first time a fade needs it. Extends 0020; edits nothing. Unverified: `check` has not run, and `MediaSession.setPlayer` mid-playback, the notification refresh, audio focus handover and the blend timing are for the owner's phone and the `deviceCheck` test (`TwoPlayerCrossfadeEngineTest`, not run). Not accepted until `check` and the phone checks pass.
