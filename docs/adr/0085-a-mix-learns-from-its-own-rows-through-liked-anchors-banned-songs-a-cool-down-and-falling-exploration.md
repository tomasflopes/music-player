# 0085: A mix learns from its own rows: liked anchors, banned songs, a cool-down and falling exploration

Date: 2026-11-21 · Status: proposed

**Context.** Slices 1 and 2 build and refill a mix from its seeds alone (ADR 0076, 0078). Design §7.6 and the plan (decisions 1, 10) want the mix to learn: songs that went well become anchors, songs that went badly stop coming, and exploration falls as feedback arrives. A mix's score is a pure function of the `play_event` rows with that `mix_id` (ADR 0079: appended songs log as `mix`, so refill batches feed it).

**Decision.**
- **`MixScoreBoard`** (`:core:taste`, `mix` package): `suspend fun of(mixId, now): MixLearning`. `OnDemandMixScoreBoard` reads `PlayEventSource.observeSince(horizon).first()` once (horizon = the decay horizon, 240 days), keeps the rows of `mixId` in memory and scores them with a `TasteScorer` built with `skipShare = 1.0` (ADR 0084), so inside its own mix a skip counts in full. Nothing is stored. **Trigger to revisit:** if one call measures slow on the full log, add a `mix_id` index or a port method that filters in the query; not a score table.
- **`MixLearning`** (plain data, so the engine stays pure): `liked` (mix score ≥ `likedMinScore` 0.5, best score first, then most recent; not gated, not capped), `banned` (score ≤ `bannedScore` -3.0), `playedInMix` (songs with a row of this mix in the last `mixRepeatDays` 14, any outcome), `informativeRows` (non-neutral outcomes that are not burst-softened). `MixLearning.EMPTY` is a mix with no feedback.
- **Liked anchors.** After the seeds' anchors, the engine adds liked songs in order, but only those whose rank key against the **seed-only** anchors is ≤ `likedGate` (R), then keeps at most `likedMax` (15): the gate first, then the cap, so a far song never uses a slot. Because the gate is measured against the current seeds, a seed edit drops the anchors that no longer sit near them.
- **R = 1.5.** Measured on the owner's library (14,165 profiled songs, ADR 0068 vector, `AnchorQuery` key = distance to the nearest anchor over that anchor's 50th-neighbour distance) for five 3-to-5-seed mixes built from real songs and artists (hip-hop, mixed styles, three artists plus a song, one tight sound, a wide five-song mix). Songs with key ≤ 1: 150 to 251 (1.1 to 1.8 %); ≤ 2: 2,628 to 4,918 (19 to 35 %); ≤ 3: 7,461 to 11,581 (53 to 82 %). The key at rank 600, the edge of the exploration band, is 1.20 to 1.38, so everything a first batch can play has a key of about 1.4 or less. R = 1.0 would admit only the nearest 150 to 250 songs and would refuse liked exploration picks (ranks 250 to 600), so exploration could not teach the mix. R = 2.0 admits a fifth to a third of the library and would never bind. R = 1.5 (about rank 1,500; key at rank 1,500 is 1.47 to 1.73) takes in the whole first-generation band and refuses songs more than one hop of drift away. It is a starting value in `MixConfig`; retune it with the slice 4 debug screen.
- **Banned songs** are removed from the candidates, which includes the opening songs, until their score decays above the threshold. A banned seed song stays an anchor (the owner chose it) but is not played.
- **Cool-down.** A song in `playedInMix` gets `mixRepeatPenalty` (+1.0) added to its rank key, so a liked song steers its neighbourhood without replaying itself. The penalty is soft: a song within the exploration band after the +1.0 can still be drawn as an exploration pick. There is no bonus for mix score.
- **Exploration** = `max(0.10, 0.30 - 0.20 * informativeRows / 200)` (`MixConfig.explorationShareFor`, from `explorationShare`, `explorationFloor`, `explorationRows`); it never rises above `explorationShare`. `RadioBatchBuilder` is constructed **per call** with that share (`MixConfig.builderConfig(share)`); the radio's own builder and numbers are unchanged.
- **API.** `MixEngine.pick` and `refill` take a `MixLearning` and stay pure. `StartMix` and `MixRefillSource` read the score board inside their existing `withContext(Dispatchers.Default)`; `MixRefillSource` reads it at every refill, so a skip a minute ago shapes the next batch. All numbers are in `MixConfig` and validated at construction.

**Deferred.** A proximity penalty near banned songs in sound space (skip learning that generalises past the song itself). Decide it with the slice 4 debug screen, once skip rate per week shows whether a ban alone is enough.

**Consequences.**
- An empty `MixLearning` reproduces the slice 2 batch exactly (pinned by golden lists for `pick` and `refill`).
- A completed play (+1, half-life 30 days) keeps a song liked for about 30 days; a ban needs about 1.5 early skips in the last weeks and fades with the same decay.
- Every batch costs one pass over the mix's rows from up to 240 days of log, scored in memory.
- A mix with no rows pays nothing: the board returns `EMPTY`.

**Tests.** `MixEngineLearningTest` (gated liked anchors, gate before cap, seed edit drops stale anchors, cool-down, banned songs including opening songs and refill, exploration schedule, golden slice 2 batches), `OnDemandMixScoreBoardTest` (fake `PlayEventSource`: filter by mix, thresholds, cool-down window, informative rows, burst softening, full-weight skips), `MixConfigTest`, and the consumer tests of `StartMix` and `MixRefillSource`.
