# 0092: Transitions are planned by a pure planner over registered techniques, the crossfade being the last one

Date: 2026-11-22 · Status: proposed

**Context.** Phase 7 (design `phase-7-dj-transitions.md`) picks, per pair of songs, between a beat match, a talking-outro entry and the phase 6 crossfade, and later adds more. ADR 0061 already owns the two-player handover and ADR 0062 the hard-cut rules and the log rules; CLAUDE.md forbids a `when` over techniques and asks that the old path stays for comparison. No new profiler version, no Mac re-run and no schema change are allowed (ADR 0065 stores the data).

**Decision.** `TransitionPlanner` in `:core:model` (pure Kotlin, no Android) runs `CrossfadePolicy` first. With the Crossfade style, or when the policy says hard cut, its decision is the plan and no technique is asked, so phase 6 is unchanged. With the DJ mix style it asks the `TransitionTechnique`s in `priority` order and takes the first plan that fits both songs; if none does, the phase 6 decision stands. Techniques are `@IntoSet` providers in `PlaybackModule`: `BeatMatchTechnique` (20) and `CrossfadeTechnique` (1000, always answers); a new technique is a new class and one line. A `TransitionPlan` is data: `HardCut`, `Fade`, or `Overlap` (switch point, incoming offset, overlap, speed, ease-back, `GainCurve`). `TransitionConfig` holds every number with the spec defaults; the style (Crossfade, default, or DJ mix) is a field of `CrossfadeSettings`, stored in DataStore. The profile reaches the planner as `TransitionProfile` through the `TransitionProfileSource` port (Room reader over the 132-byte head of ADR 0069; no audio is decoded). Planning runs off the main thread when the engine plans the next transition; the beat grid is `BeatGrid` (beat k at first beat + k * 60 / tempo, bars of 4, phrases of 16).

The engine side extends 0061 instead of forking it: `PlannedTransition` carries the overlap and its phase 6 fallback; `TwoPlayerCrossfadeEngine` preloads the spare at the incoming offset, starts when the outgoing song reaches the switch point, ramps both volumes with the plan's curve from the incoming position, and runs the phase 6 plan when the spare is not ready or the switch point has passed. Both players return to speed 1.0 after the overlap, a skip, a pause, a seek or a kill. Slice 1 beat-matches only tempos within 1.5 % (incoming at 1.0, no stretch).

**Consequences.** Extends 0061, 0062 and 0065; edits none. Slice 1 can flam by up to the tempo gap times the overlap, which the lab measures. Not accepted until `check`, `deviceCheck` and the phone checks pass; none has been run by the session that wrote this.
