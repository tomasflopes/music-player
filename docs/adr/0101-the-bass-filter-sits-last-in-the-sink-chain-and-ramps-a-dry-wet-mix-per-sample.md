# 0101: The bass filter sits last in the sink chain and ramps a dry/wet mix per sample

Date: 2026-11-23 · Status: proposed · Owner decision 1 of slice 3 · Extends 0100

**Context.** The bass swap needs a filter in each player's audio path and a way to move its gain without a click. Media3's `DefaultAudioSink` runs custom processors before its silence skipper and `SonicAudioProcessor` (speed), so a plain `setAudioProcessors` would put the filter before the time-stretch. Changing biquad coefficients every block was simulated first (44.1 kHz, 20 ms ramp, 64-frame blocks, a 40 to 150 Hz tone at 0.5): going from -24 dB to 0 dB made sample-to-sample jumps of 0.07 to 0.12, about 25 times the tone's own slope, although the dB ramp itself was smooth.

**Decision.** (1) Placement: last in the chain, after Sonic, through a small `AudioProcessorChain` that wraps `DefaultAudioProcessorChain` and appends the filter, so it sees the stretched signal. (2) Smoothing: `BassShelf` (`:core:model`) keeps one shelf fixed at full cut (`bassCutDb`, 150 Hz) and mixes it with the dry signal, `y = x + m (shelf(x) - x)`. The plan's gain in dB becomes the mix that gives that gain below the corner; the mix moves one sample at a time over 20 ms. Coefficients never change, so the state is always valid. The same simulation gave a largest step equal to the tone's slope, both ways, from 40 Hz to 1 kHz. At mix 0 with target 0 `process` returns without touching the samples (bit-exact, no work). The engine only sets a target; the ramp also covers every reset (skip, seek, pause, kill), which would otherwise be a hard step. A flush snaps to the target, since the audio is discontinuous there anyway, so a spare player preloaded with the cut starts cut.

**Consequences.** The ramp is linear in amplitude, not in dB, so between the ends the cut at mid frequencies is a blend, not a shelf of the intermediate gain; at and below the corner the gain is exact. Bypass inside Media3 is a pass-through copy, not an inactive processor, because a processor's active state is only read at a flush (ADR 0102). Corner 150 Hz and depth -24 dB are `TransitionConfig` numbers; the phone check decides them. Checked by tests run outside Gradle (see phase 7 file); not accepted until `check` and the phone checks pass.
