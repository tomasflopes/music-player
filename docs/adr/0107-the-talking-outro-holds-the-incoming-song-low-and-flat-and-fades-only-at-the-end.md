# 0107: The talking outro holds the incoming song low and flat and fades only at the end

Date: 2026-11-23 · Status: proposed (accepted only after a phone listen of lab pairs 1 to 3) · Extends 0104 (supersedes its volume curve)

**Context.** The owner listened to the talking outro of ADR 0104 on the phone on 2026-11-23: nice, but the incoming song is too loud. As built, the incoming song rises to full level over one beat and stays there under the talk, while the outgoing song is only ducked 6 dB, so the song competes with the talk for the whole tail. The owner wants it lower and steady while the outro plays out, and the real fade only at the end. (The request named ADR 0106, which is taken by the talk-on-for-listening decision; this is 0107.)

**Decision.** `TalkCurve` gets a held level. The incoming song rises over one beat to `talkHeldDb` (-12 dB, gain 0.25), holds it flat for the whole talk, and only over the last `talkFadeBars` (2 bars, was 4, about 5 s at 95 BPM) rises from the held level to 1 on a quarter sine, while the outgoing song falls on its quarter cosine over the same stretch, so it ends at 1 as the engine expects. The duck stays at -6 dB over one bar. The entry ends no later than the fade starts, so a slow beat cannot run into the fade. A held level above 0 dB is clamped to 0. `talkLevelMatch` (on) lowers the held level further when the incoming body is louder than the outgoing body: `20 log10(outgoingBodyMean / incomingBodyMean)` on the stored per-second RMS (intro end to outro start), clamped to `[-talkMaxMatchDb, 0]` with `talkMaxMatchDb` 6, so a loud song is pulled down by at most 6 dB and a quiet one is never raised (the player cannot go above 1). It needs no new profiler version: the energy curve is already read (ADR 0105); a missing curve means no match.

**Consequences.** The numbers are first guesses, tuned in the lab on the three talk pairs. The match only moves the held level: the song still ends alone at full volume, so a loud song that follows a quiet talk outro is not matched after the fade. If -12 dB is still loud, lower `talkHeldDb`; if the fade feels late, raise `talkFadeBars`. Tested on synthetic curves and virtual players; not yet listened to.
