# 0094: The incoming song is stretched up to 8 %, and half and double tempo are read from the stored beat tempo

Date: 2026-11-22 · Status: proposed

**Context.** Slice 1 matched only tempos within 1.5 % and kept the incoming song at speed 1.0 (ADR 0092). Phase 7 slice 2 stretches the incoming song with Media3 `PlaybackParameters(speed)`, which keeps the pitch, up to `maxStretch` 0.08, and tries half and double tempo. ADR 0065 stores the beat tempo, the first beat and three tempo candidates, but the first beat is the phase of the beat tempo only.

**Decision.** `TempoMatcher` (pure, `:core:model`) reads the incoming beat tempo at x1, x0.5 and x2 against the stored outgoing tempo and takes the reading with the smallest |r - 1|, where r = outgoing tempo / (incoming tempo x multiplier); ties go to x1, then x0.5, then x2. It matches when |r - 1| is at most `maxStretch` (+1e-9 so that 108 over 100 is on the limit). The incoming song plays at speed r, so its beats fall on the outgoing beats. The tempo candidates are not tried: an off-grid candidate (a 3:2 peak, a spurious peak) has no stored first-beat phase, so a match on it could not be lined up and would flam, which is worse than a crossfade; the candidates that are x0.5 or x2 of the beat tempo are already the x0.5 and x2 readings. The incoming `BeatGrid` is built at the matched tempo (beat tempo x multiplier) from the stored first beat; at x0.5 a bar is 8 native beats. The overlap length stays 8 bars of the outgoing tempo in real time; the incoming song covers r times that of its file, so the intro fit, `TransitionPlan.Overlap.incomingSpanMs` and the planner's fit check use that span. The overlap carries both grids for the engine's phase lock. `unstretchedTolerance` is removed from `TransitionConfig`. The engine reads progress as (incoming position - offset) / (overlap x r), so the volumes follow the position, not a timer (ADR 0061); the watcher wakes on the switch point instead of on a 50 ms tick, so the start is not up to 50 ms late, which would be a phase error of the same size.

**Consequences.** Extends 0092 and 0065; edits neither. Candidates stay in `TransitionProfile` for a later rule. A tempo estimate that is wrong by 1 % flams about 8 ms per second over the overlap, which is what phase lock (0096) is for. Not accepted until `check`, `deviceCheck` and the phone checks pass; none has been run.
