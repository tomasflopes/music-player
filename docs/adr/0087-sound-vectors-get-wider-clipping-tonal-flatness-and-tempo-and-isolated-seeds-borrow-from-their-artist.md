# 0087: Sound vectors get wider clipping, tonal flatness and tempo, and isolated seeds borrow from their artist

Date: 2026-11-22 · Status: proposed · Supersedes 0068 (dimensions, clipping, weights); amends 0072, 0073

**Context.** The owner found odd sound neighbours: Yung Juse "Paranóia" (a hard song) got old, melancholic boom-bap. Cause, measured on the 14,165-song Mac export: its centroid (2,905 Hz) is above p99 and its bass ratio (0.40) below p5, so p5/p95 clipping put it on the same edge point as every bright, thin song, and the weaker dimensions decided the list. With wider clipping it is one of the most isolated songs in the library (10th-neighbour distance at p96, not p29). The owner compared five variants on 12 seeds (`scripts/similar-parity` seeds + Paranóia) and chose V4 for all of them. Rejected after testing: zero-crossing rate (r = 0.86 with centroid), mean loudness, a zero-crossing "noisiness" residual (it catches tape hiss as much as distortion).

**Decision.**
- `SoundVectorizer` makes **seven** dimensions, in this order: spectral centroid, bass ratio, onset density, beat strength (sqrt after scaling), relative dynamics, **tonal flatness** (entropy of the 12 chroma bins normalised to sum 1, divided by ln 12; a chroma sum of 0 gives 0), **tempo** (log2 of the stored folded tempo, a tempo below 1 counts as 1). All from the first 33 floats, so no profiler change and no new Mac run.
- Each dimension is clipped to **p1..p99** (was p5..p95) and scaled to 0..1; weights `[1.0, 1.0, 0.5, 0.8, 0.8, 0.6, 0.4]`. Plain Euclidean distance, as before. All in `SoundVectorizerConfig`.
- **Outlier guard** (`IsolationGuard`, `:core:model`, with `IsolationGuardConfig`): a seed is isolated when its 10th-nearest distance is above the p90 of the same distance over a deterministic sample of up to 1,500 songs of the space (about 0.214 on the Mac export), computed once per `SoundSpace`. For an isolated seed, 30 % of the neighbour slots (3 of 10) go to the nearest songs whose main or featured artists include the seed's main artist, and the rest stay the nearest by sound; then everything is ordered by distance. Used by `SoundNeighbours` (both evaluation tools) and by the nearest part of song radio (the per-artist cap and gap still apply). Discover and mixes are not changed: their keys are already normalised by local density (0071, 0076).
- Artists come from the song rows in the app; `analyser similar` parses the file name (`Artist - Title feat. A, B`), as it has only paths.

**Consequences.** About 76 % of all top-10 lists change (the owner accepted this after reading them). Tempo is noisy for hip-hop (half and double tempo); its weight is low on purpose. The V4 lists in `testdata/similarity/v4-reference-lists.json` are the new reference: the parity check passes when every seed keeps at least 9 of its 10 songs. The guard brings in artist knowledge, not sound; the real fix for "hard versus melancholic" is profiler v2 (crest factor, spectral flatness, flux), not decided here.
