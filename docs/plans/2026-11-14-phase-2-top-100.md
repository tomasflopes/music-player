# Phase 2, slice 1: Top 100 that follows current taste (Implementation Plan)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans (inline, one session). Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Status:** in progress on branch `phase2/top-100` (from `main` at `f402bf7`, tag `phase-1`). Nothing is committed: the owner says when. The owner runs `./gradlew check`; the author of this plan cannot run Gradle, so every task ends with a read-through, and section 4 lists what stays unverified.

**Goal:** A Top 100 Home card whose order follows the last weeks of listening (signed weights, skip softening, 30-day half-life), built on a listening log that has one row per track the player actually moved to.

**Architecture:** The log is fixed in `:playback` (a small `PlaybackLog` maps queue moves to rows). The scoring rules are pure Kotlin in `:core:taste`, run over log rows on demand behind a `ScoreBoard` port, so a cached implementation can replace the on-demand one later by changing one binding (ADR 0023). `TopHundredProvider` is a `DynamicListProvider`; Home already draws one card per provider, so no UI code changes.

**Tech stack:** Kotlin, Room (read query only, no schema change), Hilt, JUnit 6 + Turbine. No new dependencies.

**Spec:** `design/2026-11-13-music-player-design.md` in the claude.ai project: §7.3 (log), §7.4 (signals), §7.7 (Top 100), §10 (phase 2). Phase 1 plan format: `docs/plans/2026-11-13-phase-1-mp3-player.md`.

**Path shorthand:** `{m}` = `src/main/kotlin/dev/tomasflopes/musicplayer`, `{t}` = `src/test/kotlin/dev/tomasflopes/musicplayer`, `{a}` = `src/androidTest/kotlin/dev/tomasflopes/musicplayer`.

## 0. Decisions

Approved by the owner on 2026-11-14 (brainstorm): **score on demand behind a port** (ADR 0023). The rest are calls made by the author and shown to the owner; none was objected to.

- **D1. On demand, not cached.** `OnDemandScoreBoard` reads log rows from the last `horizonDays` and scores them. No migration, nothing to go stale, a weight change applies at once. Revisit (ADR 0023 says when): recompute measured over about 150 ms on the phone, or a second consumer needs scores for every song (Discover, Rewind, smart shuffle).
- **D2. `TasteConfig` lives in `:core:taste`**, defaults in code, no Settings entry (ADR 0024).
- **D3. Every track the player moves to is logged** (ADR 0025). Queue jump counts as `picked`; next and previous as `nav`; auto-advance, repeat and error skips as `auto`. Previous that only restarts the song logs nothing.
- **D4. Song duration for the log comes from the library**, not from `player.duration`, which is unset right after `prepare()`.
- **D5. Rules (spec §7.4 plus author calls):**
  - Event score = start bonus + end weight. Picked +3, except when skipped inside 15 s.
  - Completed +1 only when shuffle was on or the source is mix or radio. Any play over 80 % counts as completed, whatever `ended_by` says. Completed also clears the song's earlier negative contributions, always.
  - Skips: under 15 s -2; from 15 s to 50 % -1; 50 % to 80 % 0. `stopped` under 80 % is 0.
  - Burst: 3 or more consecutive log rows that are skips under 15 s, starts at most 60 s apart (`burstMaxGapMs`, author call), each weighted x0.1.
  - Unknown duration (rows written before D4): `completed` counts, skipped under 15 s is -2, anything else is 0.
  - Song scores only; artist, mix and feature scopes and playlist adds wait for the phases that use them.
  - Top 100 = songs with score above 0 that are in the library, best first, ties by latest play then id. A fresh install shows a "0 songs" card.

## 2. Review focus

1. Phase 1 rows already on the phone have `song_duration_ms = 0` for first songs and `listened_ms = 0` for their completions: no division by zero, no wrong score (Task 3 tests).
2. Empty log: the board emits an empty list, the card says "0 songs", nothing crashes (Tasks 4 and 5 tests).
3. Two rows with the same `started_at`: order follows the log, so scores and bursts are deterministic (Task 3).
4. A song with a high score that has left the library: skipped, and the list is still filled to 100 from the next ones (Task 5).
5. A listened time longer than the duration (one row spanning repeat-one loops in phase 1): counts as completed (Task 2).

### Task 2: `TasteConfig`, signal rules, decay

**Files:** create in `core/taste/{m}/core/taste/`: `config/TasteConfig.kt`, `score/PlayOutcome.kt`, `score/SignalRules.kt`, `score/Decay.kt`; tests in `core/taste/{t}/core/taste/score/`: `SignalRulesTest.kt`, `DecayTest.kt`.

**Interfaces:**
- `data class TasteConfig(val weights: SignalWeights = SignalWeights(), val burst: BurstRule = BurstRule(), val decay: DecayRule = DecayRule(), val top: TopListRule = TopListRule())`. `SignalWeights`: `picked 3.0`, `completed 1.0`, `skipEarly -2.0`, `skipMid -1.0`, `skipLate 0.0`, `earlySkipMs 15_000`, `midSkipFraction 0.5`, `completedFraction 0.8`. `BurstRule`: `minSkips 3`, `softening 0.1`, `maxGapMs 60_000`. `DecayRule`: `halfLifeDays 30.0`, `horizonDays 240` (8 half-lives, under 0.4 % left). `TopListRule`: `size 100`, `minScore 0.0` (exclusive).
- `enum class PlayOutcome { COMPLETED, SKIPPED_EARLY, SKIPPED_MID, SKIPPED_LATE, NEUTRAL }`.
- `class SignalRules(config: SignalWeights)`: `fun outcomeOf(event: PlayEvent): PlayOutcome`, `fun weightOf(event: PlayEvent, outcome: PlayOutcome): Double` (start bonus plus end weight, D5).
- `class Decay(rule: DecayRule)`: `fun factor(ageMs: Long): Double` (0.5 raised to age over half-life; negative age counts as 0).

- [ ] **Step 1: Tests.** SignalRules: `completed under shuffle is 1`, `completed under mix is 1`, `completed album track in order is 0`, `picked completed under shuffle is 4`, `picked completed in order is 3`, `skip at 14_999 ms is -2`, `skip at 15 s of 200 s is -1`, `skip at 49 percent is -1`, `skip at 50 percent is 0`, `play at 80 percent counts as completed even when skipped`, `picked then skipped inside 15 s gets no picked bonus`, `stopped under 80 percent is 0`, `stopped at 90 percent is completed`, `unknown duration: completed counts, skipped at 20 s is 0, skipped at 5 s is -2`, `listened longer than duration is completed`. Decay: `1 at age 0`, `0.5 at 30 days`, `0.25 at 60 days`, `future age clamps to 1`.
- [ ] **Step 2: Implement.** Outcome first (completed by `ended_by` or fraction, then stopped, then skip bands), weight second.
- [ ] **Step 3: Read-through** of each number against `TasteConfig` and design §7.4/§13: no literal outside the config.

### Task 3: Bursts and the scorer

**Files:** create `score/BrowsingBursts.kt`, `score/SongScore.kt`, `score/TasteScorer.kt`; tests `BrowsingBurstsTest.kt`, `TasteScorerTest.kt`.

**Interfaces:**
- `data class SongScore(val songId: SongId, val score: Double, val lastPlayedAtEpochMs: Long)`.
- `class BrowsingBursts(rule: BurstRule)`: `fun softened(events: List<PlayEvent>, outcomes: List<PlayOutcome>): BooleanArray` (true where the row is in a burst).
- `class TasteScorer(config: TasteConfig = TasteConfig())`: `fun score(events: List<PlayEvent>, nowEpochMs: Long): List<SongScore>`. `events` are oldest first, as logged. Result is every song with an event, best first, ties by `lastPlayedAtEpochMs` descending then song id ascending.
- Algorithm the signature does not fix: one pass over the events; per song keep a positive sum and a negative sum, each contribution already multiplied by `Decay.factor(now - startedAt)`. A `COMPLETED` outcome sets that song's negative sum to 0 before adding its own weight. Soften a burst row's negative weight by `softening`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Tests.** Bursts: `three quick skips in a row are softened`, `two are not`, `four are`, `a long play between breaks the run`, `a gap over 60 s breaks the run`, `the song played after the burst is not softened`. Scorer: `one completed shuffle play now is 1`, `the same play 30 days ago is 0.5`, `an isolated early skip is -2`, `a burst skip is -0.2`, `completed clears that song's earlier skips but not another song's`, `a skip after a completion still counts`, `same input scores the same every time`, `rows with equal started_at keep log order`, `empty log gives an empty list`, `a row in the future never counts for more than its weight`, `ties rank by latest play then id`.
- [ ] **Step 2: Implement.** **Step 3: Read-through** that scoring needs nothing but the rows and `now` (no stored state).
